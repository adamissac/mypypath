      Unit access

      How units open for this class

      None of these cancel an assignment. An open course still owes whatever you set, and missed work still shows as late.

      A student who opens a lesson in a unit that is shut to them sees a page saying why, and what they can do instead. The lesson is not shown to them — not the exercises and not the text. What that does not do: lessons are pages of this site, and it is this site, running in the student's own browser, that declines to show one. It keeps a class out of a unit you have not opened. It does not put the material beyond reach of a student who goes looking for the page directly.

      Exercise solutions

      Turning this off removes the Show Solution button from every exercise for this class. It does not remove the answer: solutions travel inside the lesson page, so a student who opens the page source can still read one. Treat it as taking the answer key off the desk, not as locking it away.

      Test retakes

      Applies to students in this class only. Anyone learning on their own keeps unlimited retakes. A student who runs out keeps their best score — the limit stops new attempts, it never takes away one that already happened. This is enforced by the site in the student's browser, not by the database: a determined student could get past it, the same way they could read a solution out of the page source.

          Closing this class

          Archiving stops new students joining and keeps everything readable. A year after that, you can purge it: that permanently deletes every student's activity record and mirrored work for this class. Their own progress and their own saved code are never touched.

          Export and reports

          A spreadsheet of the grid above, or a week of it written out to paste into an email. All of them are built in your browser and uploaded nowhere.

          Excel gives you three tabs in one file — the grid, one row per assignment, and the roster with certificates — with the header row frozen. CSV is one flat table, and is the one to pick if something else has to read the file.

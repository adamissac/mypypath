# Trail cleanup and Python for Data cockpit

**Status:** Design approved in chat; awaiting written-spec review
**Date:** 2026-10-13
**Order:** First of three (then `2026-10-13-summit-diorama-design.md`, then `2026-10-13-unit-pages-design.md`)

## Goal

Take the rocket out of the home page trail, and put the spaceship idea on the
Python for Data page instead, as a cockpit you arrive in on purpose.

The owner's words: the liftoff that plays after the trail reaches Unit 10 is
"so bad"; the version before it (6c254aaf: cockpit instruments, altitude
weather, unit scenes) was "a little better, but a little too much"; and the
Foundations map should not turn dark between Unit 1 and Unit 10.

## Home page trail

### Remove

- The liftoff dialog (`.trail-launch`): countdown, rocket, flame and
  `renderLaunch()`. Generated by `launchScene()` in
  `scripts/trail-expedition-scenes.mjs`.
- The "Ready to explore Python for Data?" dialog (`.trail-transition`) and
  `requestDataTransition()` / `stayOnFoundations()` / `dataAccepted`. The trail
  no longer stops at Unit 10 to ask anything. Scrolling carries on into Units
  11-20.
- The progressive darkening: `.expedition__sunset`, `.expedition__night`,
  `.expedition__stars`, `is-night-altitude`, and `renderExpedition()`'s fades.
  The Foundations map keeps its daylight (`--day-*`) palette from Unit 1 to
  Unit 10, in both site themes.
- `is-trail-launching` and every CSS rule that only served the above.

### Keep

- The seam title card that closes over the map between Unit 10 and Unit 11
  while the scene changes underneath. It is now the only course transition.
- Python for Data's own night palette (`--night-*`) for Units 11-20. That is
  the course's look, not a fade.
- The course switcher, resume-on-last-course (`pypath-course`), every unit
  link, keyboard behaviour, phone camera and reduced-motion behaviour as
  described in `.claude/skills/pypath-trail/SKILL.md`.
- The founders' visor reveal (`.home-person`), which is outside the trail.

### Generator and checks

`scripts/build-trail.mjs` and `scripts/trail-expedition-scenes.mjs` own this
markup; the trail region of `index.html` is regenerated, never hand-edited.
`tests/trail-expedition.test.js`, `tests/browser/trail_transition.mjs`,
`tests/browser/trail_invariants.py` and `scripts/verify-keyboard.mjs` currently
expect the dialogs; they are updated to assert the opposite: no dialog opens
anywhere on the track, and progress past the seam lights Unit 11 directly.

The unversioned `<image href="/assets/img/summit.png">` inside the launch
scene goes with it, which also ends the double download of `summit.png`.

## Cockpit at the top of /data.html

Replaces the plain heading block (`<section class="section reveal-up">` with
the breadcrumb, h1 and lead). The unit cards below are unchanged.

### What you see

- A cockpit window frame (rounded struts, a little rivet detail) filling the
  hero. Through it: the moon (reusing the shapes of `data-moon.svg`), a field
  of stars at three depths, and a faint planet limb.
- The course title, lead line and breadcrumb sit on the glass as a heads-up
  display, so the h1 is still the first heading and still real text.
- Below the window, an instrument panel:
  - ten indicator lights, one per data unit, labelled 1-10. A finished unit
    glows; the current unit blinks slowly; the rest are dim. Each light is a
    link to its unit page, with the unit title as its accessible name.
  - one large **Launch** control. It goes to the learner's current data unit
    (first unfinished), or Unit 1 for someone who has not started. Its label
    says where: "Launch: Unit 3, Arrays with numpy".
- Palette: the existing `--night-*` Python for Data tokens (Midnight Galaxy
  with the marigold lamp), in both site themes.

### Motion

- Stars drift slowly; moving the pointer tilts the whole view a few degrees,
  as if looking around the cabin. Both pause when the hero is off screen.
- Pressing Launch plays about one second of liftoff: a small shake, stars
  stretch into streaks, the moon drops away. Then it navigates. Modifier-clicks
  and middle-clicks open the unit normally with no animation.
- Reduced motion: the same picture, nothing moves, Launch navigates at once.
  `npm run test:motion` must still find no animation.

### Build

- Static markup in `data.html` (it is generated by `scripts/build-data-course.cjs`;
  the cockpit is added there), CSS in a new `assets/css/cockpit.css`, and a
  small `assets/js/cockpit.js` for lights, tilt and the launch. No library.
- Progress comes from `window.PyPathLessonProgress` (lesson-progress.js),
  the same source as the unit pages, so a guest sees their own progress.
  Without it, all lights are dim and Launch goes to Unit 1.
- Both new files go in the versioned list in `scripts/bake_layout.py`.
